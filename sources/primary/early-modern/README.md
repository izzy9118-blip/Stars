# Early-Modern Primary Source Repair Corpus

This directory holds the acquisition layer for repairing the transition between the medieval and early-modern portions of `STAR-HISTORY-OF-POWER-001`.

The governing rule is **witness before synthesis**. A work is not integrated into the History of Power narrative merely because a modern edition, excerpt, or secondary account is available. The relevant primary witness must first be identified, its edition and language fixed, and its provenance recorded.

## Witness hierarchy

1. Original-language contemporary print, manuscript, charter, statute, or official record.
2. High-quality facsimile of that original witness.
3. Scholarly transcription of the same witness.
4. Translation, with translator and edition recorded.
5. Later excerpt or secondary quotation only as a navigation aid, never as the governing witness.

A modern translation may be used for reading and comparison, but direct quotations and close abstractions should remain traceable to the governing witness.

## Current repair corpus

The initial corpus covers:

- Luther and Calvin on temporal/civil government after the Reformation.
- Bodin on sovereignty.
- The Union of Utrecht and Act of Abjuration on Dutch constitutional formation.
- The VOC Charter and Grotius on chartered corporate, maritime, commercial, and war powers.
- Johan de Witt and Pieter de la Court on Dutch republican government and interest.
- The Petition of Right, Putney Debates, and Hobbes on the English constitutional crisis and sovereignty.
- The English Bill of Rights and Bank of England founding settlement on constitutional and fiscal-financial power.

## Johan de Witt rule

For Johan de Witt's 1654 `Deductie`, the governing witness is the **Dutch 1654 edition held by Utrecht University Library**, repository handle `20.500.14918/22745`.

No English translation is authorized to replace that Dutch witness. Later translations or modernized Dutch texts may be used only as aids and must remain traceable to the original Dutch passage.

## Integration rule

The existing `narratives/STAR-HISTORY-OF-POWER-001.md` and `stars/STAR-HISTORY-OF-POWER-001.yaml` are not changed during source acquisition. Narrative repair begins only after the required source witnesses for a production unit are locked.
